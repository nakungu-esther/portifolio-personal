Leoni Rossberg

Co-founder & Managing Director · Groundbreaker

Leoni Rossberg is the Co-founder and Managing Director of Groundbreaker, an organisation creating pathways into technology for young African women from financially constrained backgrounds. Through Groundbreaker Talents, she champions an approach that combines technical education, residential learning, mentorship, and professional connections. In discussing the programme, she highlights the importance of building confidence alongside practical skills and connecting learners with international technology mentors. Her work brings together educational opportunity and a commitment to a more diverse technology workforce.
